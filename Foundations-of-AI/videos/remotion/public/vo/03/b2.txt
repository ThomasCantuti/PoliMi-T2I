A search strategy is an ordering of the frontier. With a first-in, first-out queue, search spreads out in rings: breadth-first. Order by the path cost so far and you get uniform-cost search, which always extends the cheapest path. Use a stack and it dives down one branch: depth-first. Iterative deepening runs depth-first with a limit that grows by one each round. It sounds wasteful, yet it combines the strengths of both.
