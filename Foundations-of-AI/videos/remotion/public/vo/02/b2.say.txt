Take the eight-puzzle. You never write down its 181,440 configurations. You describe what a configuration is, which moves are legal, and what a move does. That description defines a huge graph without drawing it. Search then grows a tree of paths from the start, hoping to reach the goal after seeing only a tiny corner of that graph. And the formulation decides how big the graph is: for eight queens, from about ten to the fourteen states down to about two thousand.
