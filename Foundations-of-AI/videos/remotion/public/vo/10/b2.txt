A sentence is a constraint on possible worlds. "If it rains, the street is wet" rules out one kind of world: rain with a dry street. A knowledge base is the set of worlds that survive all its sentences, and a fact follows when it is true in every one of them. You can check that by enumerating worlds, or by rewriting sentences with sound rules like resolution. Both come down to one test: the knowledge base plus the negated query has no model.
