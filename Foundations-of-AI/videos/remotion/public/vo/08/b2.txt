So stop scoring positions by hand: play them out at random, many times, and count the wins. Noisy, but averages converge. Then spend the next simulation where it matters most. Each move is a slot machine: play the one that has paid best, unless another has been tried so rarely that it might be better. UCB1 balances the two. Select, expand, simulate, backpropagate, and repeat: the tree grows where the good moves are.
